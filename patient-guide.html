Booking & Your First Visit

How early should I book my appointment?

expand_more
Walk-ins are welcomed, but scheduled priority appointments are strongly encouraged to ensure zero wait times and uninterrupted one-on-one time with your doctor. You can request an appointment online, call us, or message us on WhatsApp.

What happens during my first visit?

expand_more
Your visit begins with a conversation about your concerns, goals and medical history, followed by a thorough examination. If needed, digital imaging is taken in-house. Your dentist then explains the findings and shares clear, written treatment options with timelines and costs, so you can decide in your own time. No treatment begins without your agreement.

What should I bring to my first visit?

expand_more
Please bring any previous dental OPG/X-rays, relevant medical prescriptions, and a list of current medications. If needed, fresh high-resolution digital imaging will be taken in-house.

Which doctor will I see?

expand_more
Cases are reviewed with an interdisciplinary approach, and you will be introduced to the specialist best suited to your treatment. You can meet our full team on the doctors and specialists page.

Where are you located, and is parking available?

expand_more
We are at House No. 203, Sector 10, Panchkula, Haryana 134109, opposite Anupam Sweets. The clinic frontage offers wide, private, and reserved parking bays right in front, so you will not need to search for parking. See directions and timings.

I live abroad. Can I plan treatment during a short visit?

expand_more
Many NRI patients plan treatment around a visit to India. Send us your previous X-rays and treatment history on WhatsApp before you travel, and we will help you plan realistic timings. The final plan is confirmed after an in-person examination.

Comfort & Anxiety

Will my treatment hurt?

expand_more
Treatment is carried out with local anaesthesia and gentle techniques, and you can ask to pause at any time. Most patients are surprised by how comfortable modern dentistry can be. If you feel anything during treatment, tell us straight away.

I am very nervous about dental visits. Can you help?

expand_more
Yes, and you are not alone. Tell us about your concerns when you book or at the start of your visit. We explain each step before we do it, work at your pace, and agree a simple signal so you can pause whenever you need to. Our quiet, private operatories are designed to help you feel at ease.

Do you offer sedation?

expand_more
Yes. Conscious sedation is available for appropriately selected patients and procedures, such as significant dental anxiety or longer treatments. You stay responsive throughout, and your dentist will advise whether it suits you.

Costs & Payments

How much will my treatment cost?

expand_more
The cost depends on your diagnosis, the materials chosen and the complexity of the treatment. After your examination you will receive clear treatment options with an itemised written estimate, so you know what to expect before you begin.

Do you offer payment plans, or help with insurance?

expand_more
Please ask our front desk about current payment options and whether your insurance can be used. They can also explain what documents you may need for a claim.

Safety & Hygiene

How are your instruments sterilized?

expand_more
Every reusable instrument goes through manual cleaning, cold disinfection, ultrasonic cleaning, drying, individual pouching and steam sterilization in one of our three Class-B vacuum autoclaves, and is inspected throughout. Sterilization is monitored through regular spore testing. You can read the full process on our technology and sterilization page.

Are dental X-rays safe?

expand_more
Digital imaging uses significantly less radiation than traditional film. We choose the smallest appropriate field of view and the lowest practical dose, with low-dose protocols for children, structural lead shielding and lead aprons when indicated. X-rays are taken only when they are clinically useful. Please tell us in advance if you are pregnant or think you might be.

Does the clinic keep running during a power cut?

expand_more
Yes. The clinic has complete power-backup infrastructure so essential clinical and comfort systems stay operational during power interruptions.

Is the clinic environmentally responsible?

expand_more
We reuse RO reject water for gardening and selected cleaning, and manage suction waste through an in-house disposal system.

About Common Treatments

How long does a dental implant take?

expand_more
For most patients, implant treatment takes several months from placement to the final crown, because the implant needs time to integrate with the jawbone. Some cases allow faster loading. Your surgeon will give you a personalised timeline after assessing your bone and bite. About dental implants.

Is a root canal treatment painful?

expand_more
With local anaesthesia, root canal treatment feels much like having a filling. It is usually done to relieve the pain caused by an infected tooth, and it is often completed in a single visit when the tooth allows. About root canal treatment.

Are clear aligners right for me?

expand_more
Clear aligners suit many, but not all, alignment concerns. Suitability depends on your bite, how crowded or spaced your teeth are, and your goals. After an examination your orthodontist will tell you honestly whether aligners, braces or another approach will give the best result. About orthodontics.

How long do veneers last?

expand_more
With good care and regular check-ups, veneers can last many years. The lifespan depends on the material, your bite and habits such as teeth grinding, so your dentist will advise you on protecting them. About smile design and veneers.

Are crowns and bridges made in the clinic?

expand_more
Many are. Our in-house digital lab designs prostheses on exocad and mills them on 5-axis ARUM machines, with sintering, glazing and 3D printing on site, so much of the workflow stays under one roof.

Emergencies & After-Care

Do you offer emergency dental care?

expand_more
Yes. Patients experiencing severe acute toothaches, fractured restorations, or facial dental trauma are given fast-track priority slots every working day and on Sunday mornings. Call us as early as you can so we can arrange your slot.

What should I do if a tooth is knocked out?

expand_more
Pick the tooth up by the crown (the white part), not the root. If it is dirty, rinse it gently with milk or saline, but do not scrub it. Try to place it back in its socket, or keep it in milk, and see a dentist immediately, ideally within an hour. Call us on +91 99151 04439 on your way.

When should I contact you after treatment?

expand_more
Call us if you have severe pain that is getting worse, bleeding that does not stop, a fever, increasing swelling, or a bite that feels wrong. If you have facial swelling with difficulty breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest hospital emergency department immediately. Always follow the aftercare instructions your dentist gives you.
