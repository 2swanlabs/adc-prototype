calendar_today

How early should I book my appointment?

Walk-ins are welcomed, but scheduled priority appointments are strongly encouraged to ensure zero wait times and uninterrupted one-on-one time with your doctor.

directions_car

Is parking easily available outside?

Yes. The clinic frontage offers wide, private, and reserved parking bays right in front of House 203, Sector 10, accommodating multiple vehicles without public congestion.

emergency

Do you offer emergency dental relief?

Yes. Patients experiencing severe acute toothaches, fractured restorations, or facial dental trauma are given fast-track priority slots every working day and on Sunday mornings.

folder_shared

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring any previous dental OPG/X-rays, relevant medical prescriptions, and a list of current medications. If needed, fresh high-resolution digital imaging will be taken in-house.
