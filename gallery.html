A walk through the clinic

Warm wood, soft light, private rooms.

Step inside before your first appointment. This is the route a new patient takes.

1

Stop 1

The welcome

Before anything else, the room does some of the work.

Live greenery, carefully chosen lighting and soothing instrumental music, all designed to make a dental visit feel less clinical and more reassuring.

2

Stop 2

The lounge

Hospitality, we believe, is part of healthcare.

Books and magazines, live news, and an in-house bean-to-cup coffee and tea corner. Wait in comfort, not in a corridor.

3

Stop 3

The picture

Diagnosis starts with seeing clearly.

In-house 3D CBCT, panoramic and digital radiographs let your dentist show you exactly what they see, often in the same visit.

The chair

Five private operatories. Nobody is hurried.

Supportive chairs, sound-buffered rooms and unhurried, one-to-one attention from your dentist and a trained chairside assistant.
