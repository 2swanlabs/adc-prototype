The journey of one instrument

Six stages between every patient.

Every reusable instrument follows this controlled sequence, every time, and is inspected throughout before it is released for clinical use.

verifiedSealed pouches are opened for your treatment. Anything not used is reprocessed.
  1. 1

    cleaning_servicesManual cleaning & debridement

    Every used instrument is cleaned by hand to remove debris.

    Why it mattersGerms hide in debris. Nothing can be disinfected until it is physically clean.

  2. 2

    scienceCold disinfection

    Cold disinfection follows manual cleaning.

    Why it mattersMakes instruments safe to handle through the remaining steps.

  3. 3

    wavesUltrasonic cleaning

    Ultrasonic cleaning reaches fine surfaces and joints.

    Why it mattersHigh-frequency agitation reaches the hinges and grooves that a brush cannot.

  4. 4

    airDrying

    Instruments are thoroughly dried before they are packed.

    Why it mattersA dry instrument keeps its sealed pouch clean and reliable.

  5. 5

    inventory_2Individual pouching

    Each instrument is individually pouched and sealed.

    Why it mattersThe pouch is a visible, traceable barrier between sterilisation and clinical use.

  6. 6

    sanitizerSteam sterilisation

    Sealed pouches go into a Class-B vacuum autoclave.

    Why it mattersVacuum-assisted steam reaches hollow and wrapped instruments.

Ask us anything

Six questions worth asking any dental clinic.

We would rather you asked. Here are our answers, in plain language.

1

How do you clean instruments between patients?

Our answer

Every reusable instrument goes through six stages: manual cleaning and debridement, cold disinfection, ultrasonic cleaning, drying, pouching and steam sterilisation. Instruments are inspected throughout, before release for clinical use.

2

What kind of autoclave do you use?

Our answer

Three Class-B high-temperature vacuum autoclaves, with cycles from about 10 minutes to 1 hour depending on the load, so urgent and unplanned treatments never mean shortcuts.

3

Is every instrument pouched until it is used?

Our answer

Yes. Every instrument meant for patient use is individually pouched and steam sterilised before use.

4

How is sterilisation checked?

Our answer

Daily spore testing and independent sterilisation audits.

5

How do you decide whether I need an X-ray?

Our answer

Only when it is clinically useful. We then choose the smallest appropriate field of view and the lowest practical dose, with low-dose protocols for children, structural shielding and lead aprons when indicated.

6

What happens if the power goes out?

Our answer

Nothing dramatic. Complete power-backup infrastructure keeps essential clinical and comfort systems running through power cuts.
