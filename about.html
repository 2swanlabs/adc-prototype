Our Clinical Genesis

Four Decades of Pioneering Dental Practice in the Tricity.

In 1983, when Panchkula was an emerging town, Dr. Sunil Anand set up one of the region’s earliest independent dental institutions with a resolute commitment: uncompromising hygiene, conservative tooth preservation, and transparent doctor-patient communication.

1983

The Foundation in Sector 10

Inauguration of Anand Dental Clinic, introducing patient-centric restorative protocols and early preventive education to Panchkula and Chandigarh families.

2000

Rotary Endodontics & Surgical Suites

Transitioned to complete electric rotary systems, apex locators, and multi-operatory workflow, eliminating procedural discomfort for root canal therapies.

2015

Second Generation Prosthodontic Leadership

Dr. Amar Anand (MDS Prosthodontics) and Dr. Sheena Anand (MDS Prosthodontics) integrated Swiss implantology, full-mouth rehabilitations, and high-aesthetic porcelain veneers.

2024

The 5-Operatory Digital Flagship

A high-tech medical sanctuary featuring Osstem CBCT and OPG imaging, three Class-B autoclaves, intraoral 3D scanning, an in-house digital lab with exocad design and 5-axis ARUM milling, and clear aligner protocols.

Multidisciplinary Mastery

Meet the Specialists Behind Your Care.

Responsible Practice

Patient Safety and Environmental Stewardship, Built into the Clinic.

Responsible dentistry is not a policy statement here. It is engineered into the water we use, the air in every operatory, the energy that powers us and the setting in which you are treated.

Water Purity and Infection Control

Every dental chair is supplied with RO-purified water drawn from a dedicated stainless-steel tank. The internal waterlines of each chair are maintained with an integrated disinfection system, and all clinical wastewater is treated on site before it enters the sewage network.

Controlled, Filtered Air in Every Operatory

Low-Dose Diagnostic Imaging

Environmental Stewardship

Rainwater is harvested on site and solar panels contribute to our energy supply. RO reject water is reused for gardening rather than discarded, and live planting with a green entrance completes a considered, low-impact environment.

Accessibility and Continuity of Care

Natural, soft-toned veneers replace the stark white walls associated with hospital settings, creating a composed and welcoming atmosphere. Comfort is supported by cervical pillows, noise-cancelling headphones, books, television and music, and a cafeteria open throughout clinic hours.
